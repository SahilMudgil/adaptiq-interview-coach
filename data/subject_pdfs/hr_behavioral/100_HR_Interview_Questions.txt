100 Frequently Asked HR Interview Questions for B.Tech Students

1. Personal Introduction & Background
- Tell me about yourself.
- Introduce yourself beyond your resume.
- What are your strengths?
- What are your weaknesses?
- What motivates you?
- How would your friends describe you?
- Describe yourself in three words.
- What makes you unique?
- Tell me something not mentioned in your resume.
- How do you handle stress?

2. Education & Academic Experience
- Why did you choose engineering?
- Why your specific branch (e.g., CSE, ECE, ME)?
- What was your favorite subject and why?
- Tell me about a project you worked on.
- What was your final year project?
- Did you participate in any technical fests or hackathons?
- Which subjects did you struggle with and how did you manage?
- What are your academic achievements?
- Have you taken any additional courses or certifications?
- How have you applied what you've learned in real life?

3. Internships & Training
- Did you do any internship? What did you learn?
- What tools or technologies did you use during your internship?
- How did you add value to your internship project?
- What challenges did you face during the internship?
- How is corporate work different from college work?

4. Projects & Technical Involvement
- Explain your major project in simple terms.
- What role did you play in the project team?
- How did you manage deadlines?
- What were the outcomes of your project?
- Would you do anything differently in your project?

5. Communication & Soft Skills
- Are you comfortable working in a team?
- How do you handle feedback or criticism?
- Do you prefer working alone or in a group?
- How do you resolve conflicts in a team?
- Describe a situation where you led a team.

6. Situational & Behavioral Questions
- Tell me about a time you failed.
- Describe a challenging situation and how you handled it.
- Share an example of good decision-making.
- Describe a time when you had to meet a tight deadline.
- What would you do if your teammate is not contributing?

7. Career Goals & Vision
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
- What are your short-term career goals?
- What are your long-term career plans?
- Do you plan to pursue higher studies?
- How do you stay updated with industry trends?

8. Company & Job Role Understanding
- Why do you want to work for our company?
- What do you know about our company?
- Why should we hire you?
- What do you expect from this role?
- What do you think this job involves?

9. Relocation, Flexibility & Adaptability
- Are you willing to relocate?
- Are you open to night shifts?
- Can you travel frequently for work?
- How do you adapt to change?
- Are you comfortable working in a hybrid or remote model?

10. General Knowledge & Awareness
- What are some current trends in your industry?
- What do you know about AI/ML/Cloud (relevant to your domain)?
- Whats your opinion on ChatGPT/AI in education?
- How has technology changed in the last 5 years?
- How do you keep yourself informed?

11. Resume-Related Questions
- Walk me through your resume.
- What is the most significant thing on your resume?
- Why is there a gap in your resume (if any)?
- Tell me more about your extracurricular activities.
- What achievements are you most proud of?

12. Campus Life & Extracurriculars
- What did you enjoy most about your college life?
- Were you part of any clubs or societies?
- How did you contribute outside the classroom?
- Describe your leadership experience in college.
- Did you organize any events?

13. Programming & Technical Knowledge (if relevant)
- What programming languages are you comfortable with?
- Have you done any coding projects?
- Whats the difference between Java and Python?
- What is OOP? Explain with examples.
- What is your experience with databases?

14. Problem Solving & Critical Thinking
- How do you approach problem-solving?
- Describe a time you solved a difficult problem.
- How do you prioritize tasks?
- Have you ever made a mistake? How did you fix it?
- What is your thought process before starting a new task?

15. Ethics, Values & Work Culture
- How do you define success?
- What does integrity mean to you?
- What is your attitude toward deadlines?
- Would you ever compromise ethics for results?
- How do you handle failure?

16. Salary, Joining & Availability
- What are your salary expectations?
- When can you join?
- Are you waiting for other interview results?
- What would you do if offered multiple jobs?
- Are you planning to switch careers?

17. Personality & Interests
- What are your hobbies?
- What do you do in your free time?
- How do you manage your time?
- What books or podcasts do you follow?
- If not an engineer, what would you have become?

18. Ending/Wrap-up Questions
- Do you have any questions for us?
- What are your expectations from the company?
- What did you learn from this interview?
- Are you open to feedback from this interview?
- Is there anything else youd like us to know?
